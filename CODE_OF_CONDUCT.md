# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We, as contributors and maintainers of BTBatteryLab, pledge to make participation in our community a respectful, welcoming, and harassment-free experience for everyone.

The goal of this project is to create a positive environment where people can collaborate, learn, and contribute regardless of:

- Experience level
- Technical background
- Nationality
- Ethnicity
- Religion
- Disability
- Personal identity and expression

---

## Expected Behavior

Examples of behavior that contribute to a positive environment include:

- Being respectful and professional
- Being constructive when giving feedback
- Helping new contributors
- Respecting different viewpoints and experiences
- Focusing on what is best for the project
- Assuming good intentions

---

## Unacceptable Behavior

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- Harassment of any kind
- Personal attacks or insults
- Discriminatory language
- Trolling or inflammatory comments
- Publishing private information without consent
- Any conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional environment

---

## Project Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to:

- GitHub issues
- Pull requests
- Discussions
- Documentation contributions
- Project-related communication channels

---

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing this Code of Conduct.

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, or other contributions that violate these guidelines.

---

## Reporting Issues

If you experience or witness behavior that violates this Code of Conduct, please contact the project maintainers.

All reports will be reviewed and handled fairly and confidentially whenever possible.

---

## Our Goal

BTBatteryLab exists to help people better understand their Bluetooth devices and battery performance.

We want the community surrounding the project to reflect the same values:

- Transparency
- Collaboration
- Respect
- Curiosity
- Continuous learning

---

Thank you for helping make BTBatteryLab a welcoming and inclusive open-source project.
